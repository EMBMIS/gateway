# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, criticise code and ideas rather than people, and keep discussions on topic.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any
other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the contact on [embmis.com](https://embmis.com), or by
GitHub's private reporting (the repository's **Security** tab, "Report a vulnerability", is also read for conduct
reports). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions or block participants
who break this code.
